How we work

A clear brief, regular technical communication, and engineering decisions that remain understandable after the project has finished.

Principles

Define the problem first

We establish the intended outcome, constraints, assumptions, and acceptance criteria before committing significant effort to a solution.

Work across interfaces

Mechanical, electronic, firmware, and production decisions affect one another. We review those interfaces explicitly rather than leaving them to emerge during integration.

Make decisions traceable

Important choices are recorded with their reasoning, including the alternatives considered and the evidence used. That makes later changes faster and safer.

Design for the next stage

A prototype, test rig, or production design has a different job to do. We tailor the engineering outputs to what needs to happen next.

Typical engagement

  1. 01

    Understand

    Discuss the problem, existing work, constraints, timescale, and the evidence needed to judge a successful outcome.

  2. 02

    Define

    Agree the scope, deliverables, responsibilities, and review points. Where uncertainty is high, the first phase may be investigation rather than design.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Work through the design with regular technical reviews. Risks, assumptions, and changes are raised while there is still time to act on them.

  4. 04

    Verify and hand over

    Check the result against the agreed criteria and transfer the design files, drawings, software, test evidence, and supporting notes needed for the next stage.

A practical first step

A short introductory call is usually enough to establish whether the problem suits our experience and what useful next step would look like. We will say plainly if it does not.
