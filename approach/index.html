How we work

A connected engineering process from the first requirement to tested hardware and useful production information.

Principles

Start with what already exists

We learn from the current design, test history and the people who know the technology before deciding what should change.

Engineer the interfaces

Mechanical, electronic, software and production decisions are developed together so the complete system works as intended.

Build to learn

Real parts and assemblies reveal what models cannot. We use that feedback while the design is still able to change.

Verify the result

Testing connects the finished hardware back to the performance, reliability and operating requirements that matter.

Typical engagement

  1. 01

    Learn the system

    Understand the current technology, operating environment, known problems and what the next stage needs to achieve.

  2. 02

    Set the next milestone

    Turn the objective into a practical engineering programme with clear deliverables, interfaces and measures of success.

  3. 03

    Develop and build

    Develop the complete system, source the right parts and processes, assemble the hardware and solve what integration exposes.

  4. 04

    Test and hand over

    Verify the finished system and provide the hardware, design information and production or test records the next stage needs.

From drawing to working hardware

The right process for each part

We work with specialist manufacturers for machining, fabrication, finishes, moulding and PCB assembly, selecting the process around the engineering need.

One connected feedback loop

Parts are reviewed and integrated by the engineers who designed the system, so supplier and build feedback returns directly to the design.

De-risk uncertainty early

Where the route forward is not yet clear, we start by answering the technical question that matters most. A focused investigation, calculation or test can replace an expensive assumption with useful evidence before the wider development moves ahead.

Start with the next important result

Tell us what exists today and what the product, equipment or process needs to do next. We will turn that into a practical first conversation.
