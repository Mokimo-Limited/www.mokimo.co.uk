How we work

A clear brief, explicit discipline interfaces, and engineering decisions documented so they remain understandable long after handover.

Principles

Define the problem first

We establish the intended outcome, constraints, assumptions, and acceptance criteria before committing significant effort to a specific solution.

Work across interfaces

Mechanical, electronic, firmware, and production decisions heavily influence one another. We review these interfaces explicitly rather than letting conflicts emerge during integration.

Make decisions traceable

Key architectural choices and component selections are recorded with their underlying rationale, trade-offs, and evidence. That makes future design changes faster and safer.

Design for the next stage

A proof-of-concept, test rig, or high-volume production design each serves a distinct purpose. We tailor engineering outputs directly to what needs to happen next.

Typical engagement

  1. 01

    Understand

    Discuss the problem, existing hardware or code, operational constraints, timeline, and the criteria required to verify success.

  2. 02

    Define

    Agree on scope, deliverables, team responsibilities, and review milestones. Where technical risk is high, the initial phase may focus on targeted feasibility investigation.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Execute the engineering design with regular technical updates. Assumptions, risks, and changes are identified early while options remain open.

  4. 04

    Verify and hand over

    Validate results against agreed acceptance criteria and transfer CAD models, schematics, source code, test evidence, and supporting documentation.

A practical first step

A short introductory call is usually enough to establish whether a project aligns with our technical capabilities and what a constructive next step looks like. We will say plainly if it does not.
