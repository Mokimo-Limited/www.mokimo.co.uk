Who we are

A small consultancy - deliberately so.

Mokimo is a small engineering design firm based in Sheffield, working across mechanical, electronic, and firmware disciplines. We take the time to understand a problem completely before proposing a solution.

People
Tom Green · Engineering

Tom studied Mechanical Engineering at Loughborough University while developing practical experience in electronics and firmware. After three years working on scientific equipment, medical devices and consumer products, he co-founded Mokimo. His work spans mechanical, electronic and software design, particularly where problems cross disciplinary boundaries. These experiences shaped Mokimo’s pragmatic, client-led approach: understand what needs to be achieved first, then design the most appropriate route to a practical solution.
