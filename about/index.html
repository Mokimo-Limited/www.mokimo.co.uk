Engineering kept close to the work

Based in Sheffield

Mokimo is a small engineering company developing complex products, specialist equipment and production systems. We work across mechanical, electronic and software engineering, carrying decisions through into real hardware and test.

What we are built for

A product that needs to become more reliable. A machine or test system that needs to exist. A production process that needs to become controlled and repeatable.

Our size keeps engineering decisions close to builds, tests and client conversations. The people developing the system stay involved as it becomes hardware.

People

His work spans mechanical, electronic and software design, particularly where problems cross discipline boundaries and must be resolved in the assembled system.

Built through the right network

Mokimo combines hands-on engineering, assembly and test with specialist manufacturing partners. That lets each part use the right process while keeping supplier communication, build feedback and system integration connected.

The result is a flexible route from technical intent to working hardware, without forcing every project through the same set of facilities.
