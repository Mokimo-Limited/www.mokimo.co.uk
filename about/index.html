Mokimo is a Sheffield engineering company developing complex products, specialist equipment and production systems across mechanical, electronic and software engineering. We build what we design, and we test what we build.

What we do

A product that isn't robust enough. A machine that doesn't yet exist. A process that never produces the same result twice. That is the work we take on.

01

Products and subsystems

Integrated hardware developed from technical requirements through detailed design, working builds and verification.

02

Test and production equipment

Purpose-built rigs, fixtures and automated equipment that make testing and production faster, safer and repeatable.

03

Industrialisation and pilot builds

Design refinement, supplier coordination, assembly information and test methods for dependable early production.

How we do it

From drawing to working hardware

We machine, print and assemble much of what we design in-house, and bring in specialist partners for the processes that demand it—moulding, fabrication, finishes, PCB assembly. Every part that comes back is reviewed by the engineer who specified it.

That keeps the route from design to working hardware short, without forcing every project through the same facilities.
