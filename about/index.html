Who we are

A small consultancy; deliberately so

Mokimo is a design engineering consultancy based in Sheffield, working across mechanical, electronic, and firmware disciplines. We take the time to understand a problem completely before proposing a solution.

How we work with our clients

1. Discovery call

A short conversation to understand what you are trying to achieve, your constraints, and whether we are a good fit. Book a call or email us.

2. Scoping

If there is a strong fit, we define the problem statement, deliverables, timeline, and commercial terms. We are happy to execute a mutual NDA prior to detailed technical discussions.

3. Delivery

Work is delivered as a fixed-scope project or on a retainer basis, depending on what best suits the engagement. We communicate regularly, document design choices, and raise potential risks early rather than optimistically.

4. Handover

Outputs are transferred with complete documentation and CAD/source files. We aim to leave your team fully equipped to proceed independently, unless ongoing support is part of the agreement.
