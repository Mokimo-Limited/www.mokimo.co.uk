How we work with clients

Template engagement model — adjust before publishing.

1. Discovery call

A short conversation to understand what you are trying to achieve, your constraints, and whether we are a reasonable fit. No obligation, no pitch deck. Book a call or send a message.

2. Scoping

If there is a potential fit, we agree the problem statement, deliverables, timeline, and commercial terms. We are happy to work under mutual NDA before detailed technical discussion.

3. Delivery

Work is delivered as a fixed-scope project or on a retainer basis, depending on what suits the engagement. We communicate regularly, document decisions, and flag issues early rather than optimistically.

4. Handover

Outputs are transferred with appropriate documentation. We aim to leave you able to continue without us, unless ongoing support is part of the agreement.
