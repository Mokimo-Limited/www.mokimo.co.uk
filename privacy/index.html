3. Who processes your data

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for marketing. Third parties involved in running this website are limited to:

  • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) — hosts the static site files and may process request metadata for content delivery and security. Transfers from the UK to the US are covered by the UK–US Data Bridge (the UK extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).
  • Google LLC (Google Fonts) — delivers font files; your IP address may be processed in the US, covered by the same mechanism. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Enquiries submitted through the contact form are delivered to our own business systems on private UK infrastructure. They are not passed to any other third party.

4. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have data erased in certain circumstances;
  • restrict or object to our processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format; and
  • withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these, email hello@mokimo.co.uk. We respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, telephone 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to resolve any concern directly first.

5. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. The policy in force is the one displayed at the time you use the site.
