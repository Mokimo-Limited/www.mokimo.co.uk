4. Data Subject Rights

Under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you possess the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access — to request a copy of the data we hold.
  • Right to Rectification — to request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure — to request deletion of data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restriction — to request a pause in data processing.
  • Right to Object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at hello@mokimo.co.uk. We aim to respond to all requests within 30 days.

3. Internal Data Management

We use self-hosted systems (ERPNext on private UK infrastructure) for CRM, workflow automation, and document management. An internal email-processing service classifies inbound and outbound mail via Microsoft Graph, reconciles business banking via Revolut, and may use AI-assisted tools (routed through OpenRouter, or run on our own self-hosted models) to enrich records. Automated filtering is applied to reduce transmission of sensitive content to external inference providers.

4. Automated Processing

While we use automated tools to assist in our workflows, all significant decisions are subject to human review. We do not engage in solely automated decision-making that carries legal or similarly significant consequences for individuals.
