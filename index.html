How we work

Develop, build, prove

One team carries your hardware from first sketch to commissioned system. Problems surface early—next to the parts they belong to—while they are still cheap to fix.

  1. 01Develop

    Requirements, architecture and detail design across mechanics, electronics and firmware.

  2. 02Build

    Much of what we design is machined, printed and assembled in-house; specialist partners handle the rest.

  3. 03Prove

    Integration and test against the numbers the system has to hit—then documented and handed over.
