Engineering Methodology

Develop, build, prove.

We bring engineering decisions into contact with real parts and real tests early. That keeps the work practical, exposes problems while they are still fast to solve, and creates a dependable route to production.

Phase 01 Scope

Requirements & Architecture

We learn the operating system: current tech, usage conditions, and failures. We agree on unambiguous deliverables and measurable success criteria.

→ DEFINED SCOPE & BOUNDARIES
Phase 02 Detail

Cross-Discipline Design

Mechanics, electronics, and software are engineered together around the behavior of the complete system, preventing integration traps.

→ 3D CAD, SCHEMATICS & BOM
Phase 03 Build

Fabrication & Assembly

Parts are machined, 3D-printed, sourced, and bench-assembled using specialist partners where required. Every incoming part is inspected by the engineer who specified it.

→ PHYSICAL WORKING PROTOTYPE
Phase 04 Prove

Test & Handover

Verification against agreed metrics, plus the complete design packages, operating documentation, and manufacturing drawings needed for the next stage.

→ VALIDATED EVIDENCE & REPO
