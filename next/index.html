What we do

Engineering that reaches the real world

Laboratory & Industrial

Test rigs, fixtures and automated equipment, purpose built to your needs. Accelerate your research, increase production reliability, and gain confidence in your data.

We can help you with:

  • Sensing and measurement that's accurate, reliable and easy to use
  • Mechanical fixtures, precision motion & fluid control
  • Closed-loop systems that say what actually happened, not just the intent

Product Development

Integrated hardware developed from technical requirements through detailed design, working builds, and rigorous validation.

We can help you with:

  • Turning your ideas into prototypes, and your prototypes into products
  • Understanding requirements, identifying interfaces and definining architectures
  • Detail design, from early concept sketches to production-ready packages
  • In-house prototyping, testing, measurement and iteration

Scalable Design

Design optimisation, supplier coordination, assembly documentation, and in-process validation for dependable early batch manufacturing.

We can help you with:

  • Design For Manufacture and Assembly (DFMA)
  • Detailed documentation packages for production, assembly and distribution
  • Connecting with suppliers, commissoning tooling, finding assembly capacity and supporting pilot production

Our approach

How we work

We bring engineering decisions into contact with real parts and real tests early. That keeps the work practical, exposes problems while they are still fast to solve, and creates a dependable route to production.

Scope

Requirements & Architecture

We work to understand your project: current tech, usage conditions, and challenges. We agree on deliverables, scope, and what defines success - and a strategy to work with, not against, your team.

→ DEFINED SCOPE & BOUNDARIES
Detail

Cross-Discipline Design

Mechanics, electronics, and software are engineered together around the behavior of the complete system, preventing integration traps. We maintain regular communication, keeping us on track and adapting to changing knowledge and needs.

→ 3D CAD, SCHEMATICS & BOM
Prototype

Build, Test & Iterate

We use a mix of prototype and production processes to create parts, assemble units and test every part together. Adjustments are made, designs are refined, and better iterations are built until everything works smoothly.

→ PHYSICAL WORKING PROTOTYPE
Prove

Test & Handover

We verify device performance against the agreed requirements, regulations and standards. We produce complete design packages, operating documentation and production drawings, and support you through scale-up to production.
