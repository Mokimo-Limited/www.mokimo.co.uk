What we can help with

Mokimo provides design engineering across mechanical, electronic, firmware, systems, and production disciplines. We can take a project from an early question through to a tested, documented result—or contribute at the point where specialist input is needed.

Engineering services

From question to working system

The disciplines below often overlap in practice. We bring the right combination together around the problem, whether that means developing one component or coordinating the interfaces across a complete product.

Mechanical Engineering

Product design, mechanism development, and structural analysis—from concept sketches through to production-ready outputs. We work in CAD, FEA, and at the bench, choosing the right level of analysis for the decision that needs to be made.

Electronics & Embedded Systems

Circuit design, PCB layout, and sensor integration for connected products and instrumentation. We can develop from proof-of-concept breadboards through to production assemblies, with attention to the interfaces between hardware and the wider system.

Firmware Development

Firmware for microcontrollers and connected hardware, plus supporting software such as control interfaces, configuration tools, and dashboards. We work from low-level device behaviour through to the software people use to operate and understand it.

Requirements, Systems & Process Design

Mapping workflows, identifying bottlenecks, and designing systems that let organisations work the way they actually need to. We can also help establish requirements and acceptance criteria before detailed engineering begins.

Production & Automation Systems

Custom jigs, assembly tooling, functional test rigs, and machine automation designed to support manufacturing, assist operators, and automate testing. Outputs can be designed for a single process or developed toward repeatable production use.

Ways of working

We can structure an engagement around the level of definition and flexibility your project needs.

Fixed-price projects

A defined scope, agreed deliverables, and a clear price. Best suited to projects where the problem and intended outputs can be described confidently up front.

Time and materials

Flexible support billed for the time spent. Useful for research, ongoing development, or projects where the right solution will emerge through investigation.

Ad-hoc consulting

Experienced input when you need it, from one-off design reviews and technical meetings to problem-solving sessions or a second opinion. We can also provide this support through an ongoing arrangement.
