What we deliver

Engineering for products, equipment and production

Mokimo takes complex physical technology through the work needed to perform reliably in the real world. We combine product development, specialist equipment and industrialisation capability around what the project needs to achieve.

Ways we can help

Product development

Products and subsystems

Turn a proven principle, existing design or new requirement into robust, integrated hardware ready for its next stage.

What this can involve

  • requirements, architecture and interface definition;
  • detailed parts, electronics and control software;
  • supplier-ready information and component sourcing;
  • assembly, integration and performance testing.

Typical outputs: working hardware, design files, bills of materials and test results.

Tools that make work repeatable

Test, calibration and production equipment

We design and build fixtures, rigs and bespoke equipment around the product, operator and measurement that the process actually requires.

What this can involve

  • measurement method and acceptance criteria;
  • mechanical fixtures, controls, sensors and interfaces;
  • operator workflow, guarding and service access;
  • commissioning, procedures and test records.

Typical outputs: commissioned equipment, operating information and recorded performance.

From development into production

Industrialisation and pilot builds

Develop the product, documentation, supply chain and test methods needed to build dependable early production units.

What this can involve

  • design-for-assembly and design-for-manufacture review;
  • controlled bills of materials, drawings and build information;
  • specialist supplier coordination and incoming review;
  • assembly support, unit test and build feedback.

Typical outputs: pilot units, controlled production information and a practical route to repeatable manufacture.

When performance falls short

Failure investigation and system improvement

We reproduce the problem, trace interactions across the assembled system and develop a remedy that can be tested rather than argued from first principles alone.

What this can involve

  • history capture, inspection and targeted instrumentation;
  • root-cause hypotheses and controlled experiments;
  • design or process corrections;
  • verification against an agreed failure or performance condition.

Typical outputs: a clear diagnosis, practical changes and verification of the improved system.

Connected engineering

One process from design to test

Mechanical, electronic and software decisions are developed together, then followed through the build and test process. Problems are solved in the complete system rather than handed between separate disciplines.

The right process for every part

We combine hands-on engineering, assembly and test with specialist manufacturing partners. That gives each component access to the right process while keeping design feedback, supplier communication and system integration connected.

Where Mokimo fits best

Complex physical technology

Products, equipment and production problems where mechanics, electronics, software and manufacture influence one another—and where progress depends on bringing those decisions together.

A meaningful next milestone

A product made robust, a machine commissioned, a test made repeatable, a failure understood or a pilot build completed. The best projects have a clear technical change worth making.

How projects begin

Defined project

Where the requirement is clear, we set out the work, deliverables, programme and commercial structure before development begins.

Focused investigation

Where the route is uncertain, an initial phase can answer the most important technical question and establish the right direction for further development.
