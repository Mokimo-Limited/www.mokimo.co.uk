Engineering for products, equipment and production

Product development, test and production equipment, industrialisation, and failure investigation. Every engagement ends in hardware that works and the evidence that it does.

Products and subsystems

Turn a proven principle, an existing design or a new requirement into integrated hardware that's ready for its next stage.

  • requirements, architecture and interfaces;
  • detail design across parts, electronics and firmware;
  • sourcing, assembly, integration and test.

Typical outputs: working hardware, design files, bills of materials and test results.

Test and production equipment

Fixtures, rigs and bespoke equipment designed around the product, the operator and the measurement your process depends on.

  • measurement method and acceptance criteria;
  • mechanical fixtures, controls, sensors and interfaces;
  • operator workflow, guarding and service access.

Typical outputs: commissioned equipment, operating information and recorded performance.

Industrialisation and pilot builds

The product, documentation, supply chain and test methods needed to build dependable early units.

  • design-for-assembly and manufacture review;
  • controlled bills of materials, drawings and build information;
  • supplier coordination, assembly support and unit test.

Typical outputs: pilot units, controlled production information and a practical route to repeatable manufacture.

Failure investigation

We reproduce the problem, trace it across the assembled system and develop a remedy that gets tested—not argued from first principles.

  • history capture, inspection and targeted instrumentation;
  • root-cause hypotheses and controlled experiments;
  • design or process corrections, verified against the agreed condition.

Typical outputs: a clear diagnosis, practical changes and verification of the improved system.
