Firmware development

Microcontroller firmware, control, and interfaces

Firmware is where a pile of parts becomes a machine — motion, sensing, and the interface a person actually touches, all having to agree with each other. We write it for hardware we've usually had a hand in designing, which makes the awkward questions easier to answer.

Projects

How we work

Microcontroller firmware for motion control, sensor handling, and operator interfaces, plus the supporting software that tends to come with it — configuration tools, test harnesses, and dashboards for the people who have to live with the machine.

Working across the electronics and mechanical design as well means the firmware can be written against the real system rather than an assumption about it, and that a problem can be solved wherever it is cheapest to solve — sometimes in code, sometimes with a different sensor, sometimes with a bracket.
