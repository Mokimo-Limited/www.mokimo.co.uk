Scope

Scope includes custom assembly jigs, functional test rigs, inspection fixtures, build documentation, and targeted automation. The goal is to make assembly and validation repeatable, efficient, and measurable without introducing unnecessary system complexity.

We evaluate production requirements during product development: defining assembly order, designing alignment aids, specifying fastener torques, and creating clear test procedures for operators and manufacturing partners.
