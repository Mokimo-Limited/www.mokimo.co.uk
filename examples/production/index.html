Assembly validation

Functional test rig for a rotating assembly

Problem

A rotating mechanical assembly needed repeatable validation under load before it could be signed off for production — ad-hoc bench testing wasn't giving consistent or measurable results.

Approach

Built a dedicated functional test rig to exercise the assembly through its operating envelope, making failure modes visible and measurable rather than relying on one-off manual checks.
