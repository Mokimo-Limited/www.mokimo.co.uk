Scope

Work can include assembly jigs and fixtures, functional test rigs, inspection aids, process definition, tolerance analysis, build documentation, and targeted automation. The aim is to make the expected result repeatable and measurable without adding unnecessary complexity.

Production needs are considered alongside the product design: who will assemble it, how critical features will be controlled, what must be inspected or tested, and which information a supplier or operator needs.
