Requirements, systems & process design

A clear basis for design, verification, and delivery

Defining what a system must achieve, structuring discipline boundaries, and establishing clear verification criteria before building.

Requirements engineering

Developing clear, unambiguous, and verifiable requirements at the start of a project. We help capture stakeholder needs, separate functional requirements from implementation choices, establish pass/fail criteria, and maintain traceability as designs evolve.

System architecture

Defining system boundaries, inter-subsystem interfaces, operating modes, and allocating functions across mechanical, electronic, and software domains. This provides each discipline with an explicit, stable basis for design.

Workflow & process design

Mapping technical workflows, information handovers, and operational bottlenecks. We help structure processes where engineering execution intersects with operations, quality, or commercial functions.

Standards & compliance

Navigating technical standards relevant to your product. We help interpret standard requirements, translate them into design constraints, and structure test plans to demonstrate compliance.

Mokimo does not provide certification, legal approval, or notified-body services.
