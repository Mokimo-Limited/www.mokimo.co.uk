Requirements, systems & process design

A clear basis for design, verification, and delivery

Support for defining what a product or process needs to achieve, how its parts interact, and how the result will be assessed.

Requirements

Developing and reviewing requirements so they are clear, necessary, feasible, and verifiable. This can include capturing stakeholder needs, separating requirements from design choices, defining acceptance criteria, and maintaining traceability as the design changes.

System definition

Defining system boundaries, interfaces, operating states, and the allocation of behaviour across mechanical, electronic, and software elements. The output should give each discipline a clear basis for design and integration.

Process and workflow

Mapping existing workflows, responsibilities, information flows, and handovers before defining an improved process. This is useful where technical work crosses operational, commercial, or quality functions and the current process is unclear or difficult to scale.

Standards and compliance

Where a product is subject to a technical standard, we can help identify and interpret the engineering requirements that affect the design, documentation, and verification plan.

Mokimo does not provide certification, legal approval, or notified-body services.
