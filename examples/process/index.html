Requirements, systems & process design

A clear basis for design, verification, and delivery

Requirements and systems engineering connect the performance a product needs to the decisions made during design, build and test.

Requirements engineering

Clear, verifiable requirements from the start: stakeholder needs captured, functional requirements separated from implementation choices, pass/fail criteria agreed, and traceability kept as the design evolves.

System architecture

System boundaries, interfaces, operating modes, and the allocation of functions across mechanics, electronics and software—giving each discipline a stable basis to design against.

Workflow & process design

Mapping technical workflows, information handovers and bottlenecks, and structuring the process where engineering meets operations, quality and commercial teams.

Regulatory preparation

Where it's part of the agreed scope, we identify the technical requirements that apply, translate them into design constraints, prepare verification evidence and coordinate with specialist test partners.

Mokimo is not an accredited test laboratory, certification body or legal adviser, and does not certify or approve a product.
