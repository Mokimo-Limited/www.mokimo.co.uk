Examples / Bikestow

Bikestow

Personal project · Automated post-operation machining centre

An automated post-operation machining centre featuring a front-mounted component buffer and automatic part detection, designed for continuous, unattended production runs.

Mechanical Electronics Firmware Production

Objective

Eliminate manual component loading between post-operation machining cycles. The machine needed to hold a batch of parts in a buffer, detect each component automatically, cycle the machining gantry, and advance to the next part without operator intervention.

System design

Components are loaded into an integrated front buffer. Custom controller electronics detect part presence, trigger the lead-screw gantry sequence, and manage component indexing. Mechanical layout, power electronics, and firmware sequencing were developed concurrently as a unified machine.

Mechanical design

The motion platform uses a compact, lead-screw driven gantry with custom machined aluminium bearing mounts and nut blocks, optimized for high duty cycles within a tight footprint. Workholding, chip shedding, and drive mechanics were designed together.

Electronics and firmware

A custom controller board handles motor drive, limit sensing, and sensor inputs. Microcontroller firmware manages the motion profile, part detection logic, safety interlocks, and machine status display.
