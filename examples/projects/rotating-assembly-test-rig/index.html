Rotating assembly test rig

Test and validation project

A dedicated functional test rig developed to exercise a rotating assembly under controlled load and replace inconsistent manual checks.

Production Mechanical

Objective

Establish a consistent method for assessing the assembly under load. Previous manual checks varied between tests and did not produce directly comparable results.

Approach

The rig located and drove the assembly in a repeatable configuration, applied controlled test conditions, and made the relevant behaviour observable and measurable across repeated runs.

Outcome

The assembly could be assessed against the same conditions after each design change, improving comparison between tests and making failure modes easier to reproduce.
