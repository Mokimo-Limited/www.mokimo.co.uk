Rotating assembly test rig

Repeatable validation under load

Ad-hoc bench testing tells you whether something worked once. A rig tells you whether it works every time, and gives you a number when it doesn't.

Production Mechanical

The problem

A rotating mechanical assembly needed to be validated under load before it could be signed off. Testing it by hand on the bench gave inconsistent results that were difficult to compare between units, which made it impossible to say with any confidence whether a change had improved anything.

Approach

Built a dedicated functional test rig to drive the assembly through its operating envelope in a controlled, repeatable way. Fixturing the assembly properly and applying load deliberately turns intermittent, hard-to-reproduce behaviour into something visible and measurable — which is usually the difference between chasing a fault and fixing one.

Outcome

Failure modes became measurable rather than anecdotal, and the assembly could be assessed against the same test conditions each time it was changed.
