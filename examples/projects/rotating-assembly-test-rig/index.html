Rotating assembly test rig

Test and validation project

A functional test rig designed to exercise a rotating mechanical assembly under controlled load, replacing ad-hoc manual testing with repeatable measurements.

Production Mechanical

Objective

Establish a standardized test procedure for evaluating rotating assemblies under operational torque and speed. Previous manual checks produced variable results and could not reliably catch subtle assembly defects.

Approach

The rig secures the assembly in precision alignment, drives it through a pre-programmed duty cycle under controlled resistance, and monitors mechanical feedback to detect drag, eccentricity, or binding.

Outcome

Turns subjective operator feedback into consistent, quantitative test data, enabling rapid verification after design modifications and clear identification of failure modes.
