Scope

Engineering scope spans schematic capture, PCB layout, component selection, power regulation, sensor and actuator drive circuits, prototyping, and structured bench bring-up. For electromechanical devices, board geometry, mounting features, thermal management, connector placement, and harness routing are designed in tandem with the mechanical enclosure rather than forced into available space afterward.

Developing the supporting firmware alongside hardware design allows for immediate bench validation during bring-up, ensuring signals, power rails, and peripheral interfaces perform reliably under real-world conditions.
