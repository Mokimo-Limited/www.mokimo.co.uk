Examples / Electronics

Electronics & embedded systems

Circuit design, PCB layout, and sensor integration

The electronics in a product rarely sit on their own: they have to fit inside something, drive something, and be told what to do. Most of our electronics work happens alongside mechanical and firmware design on the same project, which is usually where the interesting constraints turn up.

Projects

How we work

Schematic and PCB design, sensor and motor integration, and the bench work that follows: bringing a board up, finding out where it disagrees with the datasheet, and getting it to a state where the firmware can be trusted to be the thing under test.

Because we also do the mechanical and firmware sides, we tend to design boards with their eventual home in mind — connector placement that suits the harness rather than the layout, mounting that the enclosure can actually achieve, and test points where a person with a probe can reach them.
