Scope

Schematic capture, PCB layout, component selection, power regulation, sensor and actuator drive circuits, prototyping and structured bench bring-up. On electromechanical products, board geometry, mounting, thermal management, connectors and harness routing are designed with the enclosure—not forced into whatever space is left.

Writing the firmware alongside the hardware means signals, power rails and peripherals get validated on the bench as soon as a board exists.
